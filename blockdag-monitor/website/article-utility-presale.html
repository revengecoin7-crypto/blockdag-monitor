The aftersale that never ended: BlockDAG launches another sale

"No sale after May 7th." That was the official promise. Less than 24 hours later, on May 8, BlockDAG launched a new Utility Presale at $0.0000005 per BDAG. This is the sixth distinct sales mechanism since the original presale began in 2024.

BlockDAG Exposed · Investigations Desk· Published 11 May 2026· Based on 359,947 Telegram messages through May 11, 2026

BlockDAG ran dozens of "AFTERSALE CLOSES FOREVER" messages on May 7, 2026. An admin stated clearly: "No sale after May 7th." On May 8, BlockDAG launched a new "Utility Presale" at $0.0000005 per BDAG, marketed with "160X POTENTIAL" and "7 DAYS ONLY." The promise lasted less than 24 hours.

What was promised

Throughout May 5, 6 and 7, BlockDAG's official Telegram channel sent the message "AFTERSALE CLOSES FOREVER" dozens of times. The language was unambiguous: the aftersale would permanently end on May 7 as the casino launched. An admin response to a direct community question stated: "No sale after May 7th."

The community had heard similar language before. Since 2024, BlockDAG had run multiple "FINAL HOURS" and "PRESALE ENDING FOREVER" campaigns — each followed by a continuation or a new mechanism. This time, the community predicted exactly what would happen next.

"Aftersale is ending for the casino launch, but we cannot place bets for four days — I am guessing that there will be a CASINO SALE. It is not an aftersale, it is a casino sale." — Community member, May 5, 2026

What happened on May 8

On May 8, 2026 at 17:15 UTC, the official BlockDAG Telegram channel announced: "UTILITY PRESALE NOW LIVE. $0.0000005 per BDAG. 160X POTENTIAL. 7 DAYS ONLY. FULL ECOSYSTEM ACTIVATION."

The price of $0.0000005 per BDAG is, on paper, 244 times below the current market price of approximately $0.000081. The "160X potential" claim refers to the gap between this new presale price and the current market price — not the $0.05 launch price that was promised at the start of the original presale in 2024. At $0.05, the gap from $0.0000005 is actually 100,000 times, meaning investors buying the Utility Presale are still 99.99% away from the original promised return.

No URL was provided in the initial announcement. No technical explanation was given for why a new token sale was needed. The casino, which launched the previous day in beta form without betting functionality, was cited as the reason for the "FULL ECOSYSTEM ACTIVATION" framing.

The six BlockDAG sales mechanisms since 2024

  • Presale (2024–early 2026) — ran for 450+ days, extended repeatedly after "FINAL HOURS" warnings
  • Batches 1–5 (Feb–May 2026) — phased claim release; Batch 5 only 40% released as of May 14
  • Aftersale (launched May 2026) — positioned as the last chance; "closes forever May 7"
  • Live Swap (Apr 30, 2026) — 15% below market price, "instant wallet delivery"; deposit issues reported
  • X10Swap (May 7, 2026) — Telegram bot collecting BDAG in exchange for USDT "rewards"; community flagged as token extraction
  • Utility Presale (May 8, 2026) — $0.0000005 per BDAG; launched the day after "No sale after May 7th"

The community response

The reaction in the Telegram group was immediate and largely resigned. Several community members pointed out the pattern directly: "BDAG presale is never going to end until they take all money from the innocent people's account. Shame on your team. You failed as a project."

Others noted the mathematical impossibility of the "160X" claim. If the current market price is $0.000081 and the Utility Presale price is $0.0000005, the "160X" would only apply to someone buying the presale and selling immediately at market price — not to reaching the originally promised $0.05. Community members also questioned why, if the ecosystem was "fully activated," new investors needed to buy tokens at all.

A community member who described himself as a large investor said: "I am a large investor and have been very patient but they keep cheapening the value and these constant sales are annoying."

The "7 days only" pattern

The Utility Presale was announced with a "7 DAYS ONLY" countdown — the same urgency framing used in every previous sale mechanism. The original presale ran for over 450 days after its first "FINAL HOURS" warning. The aftersale ran until its own "CLOSING FOREVER" date. Community members immediately noted this: "After utility sale end the name will be: casino market sale, or full ecosystem activation sale."

No community member has independently verified that the Utility Presale has a hard cutoff date. The team has not explained what happens to the presale if it does not sell within 7 days.

What this means for the promise tracker

This is promise #65 on the BlockDAG tracker: "No sale after May 7th." It was broken within 24 hours. The total now stands at 60 broken promises out of 65 tracked — a 92.3% failure rate.

The broader pattern is consistent with every previous "final" deadline in the project's history. A date is set. Marketing around the date is intense. The date passes. A new mechanism appears. The previous deadline is not mentioned again.

The Utility Presale is currently running. The May 14 deadline for casino full launch and Batch 5 claims (40% only) has not yet passed as of this writing.
