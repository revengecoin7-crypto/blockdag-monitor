  • March 5, 2026 — admin: "Any FUD will be removed with zero tolerance." The phrase appeared the day after launch and has been quoted in BlockDAG's own Telegram group throughout the year.
  • May 12, 2026 — our CoinMarketCap account banned. Reinstated after appeal. The content cited was independently verifiable. The pattern is consistent: critics removed, evidence remains.
  • May 12-13, 2026 — @blockdagnetwork disables all replies on its posts. Now no community member can publicly reply to any BlockDAG announcement on X.
  • May 19, 2026 — direct block of @blockdagmonitor. The latest escalation. The blockade is now per-account, not just per-post.

Nothing. The site stays online. The promise tracker keeps tracking. The 362,417 Telegram messages we have analyzed are still indexed.
