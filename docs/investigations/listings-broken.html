"Day-one listings on 20 exchanges." Reality: three small exchanges, no DEX.

BlockDAG marketed Day-1 listings on 20 exchanges and DEX availability on launch (March 4). At launch, the token appeared on three small CEXs with no DEX route. Liquidity disclosed at the January 6 investor meeting: $32M.

BlockDAG Exposed · Investigations Desk· Published 14 Apr 2026· 5 min read· Evidence cited →

From September 2024 onward, BlockDAG's most quoted marketing line was the 20-exchange listing promise. It was pinned in Telegram. It was repeated in every interview. It made it onto the homepage.

On March 4, 2026 — the day mainnet launched and the day the listing promise had to either land or not — BDAG appeared on three small CEXs. None of them were Tier-1. There was no DEX route. Of the twenty exchanges named or implied across two years of marketing, seventeen never listed the token.

The math from the promise side

  • Promised: 20+ exchanges including all Tier-1, plus DEX availability
  • Delivered: 3 Tier-3 CEXs, no DEX
  • Promised launch price: $0.05
  • Actual opening trade: $0.000503

Why this is connected to the liquidity disclosure

To list on twenty exchanges with the marketed launch price, a project needs committed liquidity at each venue proportional to expected day-one volume. The $32M launch-liquidity figure leadership disclosed at the January investor meeting cannot support that. It can support a small number of small venues with thin order books, which is what materialized.

In other words: the listing failure and the price failure and the raise-figure walk-back are not independent. They are three views of the same fact.
