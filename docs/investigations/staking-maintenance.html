Staking has been "under maintenance" for 70+ consecutive days

Promised live February 19, 2026, BlockDAG's staking product has now been listed as "under maintenance" for ten weeks. Holders cannot deposit, withdraw, or earn the promised 25% APR. Customer support tickets routinely go unanswered.

BlockDAG Exposed · Investigations Desk· Published 19 Apr 2026· 4 min read· Evidence cited →

"Staking is now LIVE. Earn up to 25% APR on your BDAG. Withdrawals available at any time." That was the BlockDAG official Telegram channel on the morning of February 19, 2026. Within forty-eight hours, the same product page was marked "under maintenance." It has not come back.

As of the morning this story is published, the staking product has been "under maintenance" for seventy-nine consecutive days. The Help Center page describing how to stake is still live. The page on which to actually do it is not.

What holders cannot do

  • Deposit BDAG to begin earning the marketed APR
  • Withdraw BDAG that was deposited during the brief two-day window the system was live
  • Get a clear ETA for restoration of service
  • Reach customer support — current ticket-response time exceeds 90 days

What holders are told

The standard response, when one is given, is that the staking system is being "upgraded." No timeline. No technical detail. No reference to the funds currently locked. Several community members have posted screenshots of identical, copy-pasted support replies dated weeks apart.

Why this is on the promise tracker as "broken"

A product that is unavailable is, in the simplest sense, not delivered. A product that has been unavailable for the duration of its public existence is not delivered as promised. The 25% APR figure, marketed for over a year before launch, is unrealizable while the system is offline. The promise is on the tracker as Broken.
