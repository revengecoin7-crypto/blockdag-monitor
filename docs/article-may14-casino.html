May 14: the casino that still wasn't.

May 7 was supposed to be the casino launch. May 14 replaced it after the first deadline was missed 27 minutes into the announcement. On May 14, no confirmed casino launch occurred. The admin response: "please follow the pinned messages for updates."

BlockDAG Exposed · Investigations Desk· Published 14 May 2026· Based on 359,947 Telegram messages through May 14, 2026

May 14, 2026 was the date confirmed by three separate BlockDAG admin messages on May 7 as the full casino launch date. On the morning of May 14, community members asked the admin whether the casino had launched. The response: "Please follow the pinned messages for updates. Once launched, it will be shared." No casino launched. No new date was announced.

The second missed casino deadline

May 7 was the original casino launch date — the most marketed deadline in the project's history. At 18:10 UTC on May 7, the official channel declared the casino "officially LIVE." At 18:37 UTC, 27 minutes later, the same channel said "Casino will go live in 7 days." May 14 was set as the replacement date, confirmed by three admin messages.

On May 14, the same pattern repeated. At 08:23 UTC, a community member asked the admin directly: "Casino today or tomorrow?" The admin response: "Please follow the pinned messages for updates. Once launched, it will be shared." This is the same formulation used for every previous delayed delivery — staking, miners, exchange listings. The word "once" implies the launch had not yet occurred.

As of the time of writing, no community member has posted a working casino link, a screenshot of placing a bet, or any confirmation of a functional gambling product.

Batch 5 claims: launched but broken

Batch 5 claims were scheduled alongside the casino launch on May 14. The claims function did appear to open. However, immediate reports of failures emerged. Community members reported "all claims are still failing for today." At 04:00 UTC on May 14, user Mm reported: "Batch 2 claim is still zero." Technical issues with staking and wallet connections continued to be cited as preventing proper functionality.

The admin acknowledged "the technical and development teams are actively working to resolve the staking and wallet connection issues." This same statement, or variations of it, has appeared in the Telegram group for 85 consecutive days since February 19, 2026, when staking was first promised live.

Casino deadline history

  • Original promise: Casino LIVE May 7, 2026
  • May 7, 18:10 UTC: "Casino is officially LIVE" — no URL, no access, no working product
  • May 7, 18:37 UTC: "Casino will go live in 7 days" — 27 minutes after declaring it live
  • May 14 confirmed as new date by 3 admin messages on May 7
  • May 14 morning: "Please follow pinned messages for updates" — no launch confirmed
  • New casino deadline: Not announced

Community response

The community reaction on May 14 reflected two years of deadline fatigue. One member wrote: "Reminds me of a story of the little boy who cried wolf." Another stated: "No one wants a casino — they want the coin to launch before a lawsuit shuts the whole thing down." A third investor noted: "The launch price of $0.05 is gone. Count your loss."

Several users reported they had been trying to unstake their coins for two months without success. One wrote: "Been trying to unstake my coins for two months — it won't let you unstake." Another: "Still unable to unstake. Showing RPC error." The admin response to these specific reports was: "The technical and development teams are actively working to resolve the staking and wallet connection issues."

"All I see pinned from the BDAG team is always only selling more tokens. They don't give anything about anything but continuing to sell as many tokens as possible." — Community member, May 14, 2026

Where things stand on May 14

BDAG trades at $0.000081 — 99.8% below the promised $0.05 launch price. Staking has been broken for 86 days. No community member in 359,947 analyzed Telegram messages has confirmed receiving a physical miner. Batch 4 claims are scheduled for May 27. The casino launch has no new confirmed date. The Utility Presale, which was supposed to close forever before May 14, continues to run.

The count stands at 60 of 65 tracked promises broken or misleading. The pattern documented across every previous deadline — exchange listings, staking, miners, the original casino date — has now repeated on May 14.
