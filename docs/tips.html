If you have something to share — read this first.

We protect sources. Use a channel below that matches the sensitivity of what you are sending. Default to the most secure option you are comfortable with.

If you worked at BlockDAG, work there now, or have direct knowledge of something we should publish — we want to hear from you. The mechanics below are designed so that you can send us material without us being able to identify you, even if we wanted to.

Via X

Send a direct message to @blockdagmonitor on X. If your information is sensitive, create a new anonymous account before reaching out.

Via Telegram

Send a message to t.me/blockdagexposed. Use a separate Telegram account if you need anonymity.

Via email

Send to blockdagmonitor@gmail.com. If your information is sensitive, use a new email account from a network you do not normally use.

What helps us most

  • Primary documents (screenshots with timestamps, contracts, leaked materials)
  • Wallet addresses or transaction hashes — we do the on-chain work
  • Names of additional sources who can corroborate, even if you can't introduce us
  • The context that makes the document meaningful — when was it taken, who saw it, what happened next

What we will never do

  • Reveal your identity, even under legal pressure short of a court order
  • Publish a tip without independent verification
  • Pay for information
